The agent: what it is and who it serves

Angel Anchor gets investors money against their holdings at the lowest cost, then manages that credit line for its life.

Prepares and guides; the user authorises; the system executes.

Two halves of the job

The moment of need

  1. Detects or receives the need for cash.
  2. Computes the limit from holdings and haircuts.
  3. Shows the borrowing cost, and tax on selling when borrowing is cheaper.
  4. Pre-fills from existing KYC and holdings.
  5. The user reviews the KFS, e-signs and approves the pledge by OTP.
  6. Angel Fincap sanctions and the money goes to the user's own bank.

The life of the loan

  • Collateral monitoring: daily, warnings before a margin call.
  • Shortfall guidance: amount, deadline, cure options; Angel Fincap decides any invocation.
  • Repayment nudges: from dividends, sale and redemption proceeds.
  • Limit hygiene: renewals, pledges, releases; higher limits only on the user's request.

Messaging rules

  • Tax shown only when LAS is cheaper
  • Never recommends selling
  • Tax is an estimate
  • KFS before sign-off

The business problem

Revenue leans on broking

About two-thirds of income through FY25; FY26 profit fell 21.9% to ₹915 cr under F&O rules.

Distribution rides on lenders

₹6.1 bn in Q4 FY26, down ~15% QoQ; volumes follow partners' risk appetite.

Holdings sit idle

1% of custody assets drawn as LAS is ~₹17 bn, about 23% of today's MTF book.

Capital is committed

Why an agent: LAS needs RM-style servicing. Angel One has ~3,050 employees for ~40 million clients. An agent scales horizontally to serve all of them at a fraction of the cost.

The AI model never calculates. Every number comes from a rules engine and is checked before the customer sees it.

Angel One holds both ends of the pledge.

Angel One is the CDSL depository participant for its own clients. Since 15 July 2026, DPs must approve LAS pledge requests the same day, so Angel One can make that step close to instant. For mutual funds, liens go by API to CAMS or KFintech and the investor confirms by OTP.

Rollout

  1. Phase 0 · months 0–3

    Build the four engines, connect CDSL and RTA flows, run the LTV monitor in shadow mode on the beta book.

    Exit: engine figures match Angel Fincap's exactly; every real margin call caught earlier; compliance sign-off.

  2. Phase 1 · months 3–6

    Conversational journey, banners, shortfall alerts and repayment nudges for eligible users, with a human operations fallback.

  3. Phase 2 · months 6–12, gated by results

    Partner-loan offers ranked by APR; Account Aggregator income checks for higher-limit requests (purpose code 103); AP copilot, with an operations copilot as the alternative.

    Gates: zero engine mismatches; earlier alerts, few false alarms; better cure rates; complaints within target.

A state machine drives every alert

  1. Healthy

    LTV within policy: repayment nudges and limit offers.

  2. Watch

    Early-warning level: warns and shows how far prices can fall.

  3. Shortfall

    Margin-call level: states amount and deadline; offers cure options.

  4. Cured

    LTV restored by the user: confirms and returns to Healthy.

  5. Invoked

    Deadline missed or hard breach: informs the user; Angel Fincap sells per its policy.

Compliance, built in

Digital Lending Directions

KFS before sign-off, a cooling-off period, data in India, disbursal only to the borrower's account, higher limits only on request.

Multi-lender rule

Every matching offer shown, unbiased, ranked by a published metric.

DPDP Act

Notice and consent duties apply from 13 May 2027; consent notices built into the journeys now.

RBI FREE-AI

Board-approved AI policy, audit trail of engine calls, messages and authorisations.

Value: how it is monetized

  1. Immediate

    Cost savings and efficiency

    Servicing without an RM team, faster journeys, fewer forced sales.

  2. Years 1–3

    Attributable increase

    Extra LAS income and partner disbursals above a holdout baseline.

  3. Once mature

    Licensing

    To Angel One's lending partners first, then outside lenders.

  4. Indirect

    A larger book

    Co-lending and credit on UPI.

The agent claims only what it adds above a holdout baseline, never the LAS fee or partner commissions themselves.

Measurement plan

5–10% of eligible users are held out. They keep the standard LAS journey and every regulatory notice, including margin calls; only the agent is withheld.

  • Share of servicing handled by the agent
  • Resolution time
  • Cost per interaction
  • Time to disbursal

Months 3–6

  • Limit set-up rate and money drawn
  • Banner-to-application conversion
  • Alert lead time
  • Shortfall cure rate

Months 6–12

  • LAS book uplift
  • Forced-sale rate
  • Credit cost
  • Partner-disbursal uplift

Beyond Year 3

License the agent

Stage 1: Angel One's lending partners; platform fee plus a per-active-loan fee. Stage 2, an option: outside lenders.

Grow the book

Co-lending under RBI rules from January 2026; credit on UPI if NBFCs are allowed.

LAS product changes the agent amplifies

Fee on first withdrawal, rates by collateral quality, free renewal for active borrowers.
